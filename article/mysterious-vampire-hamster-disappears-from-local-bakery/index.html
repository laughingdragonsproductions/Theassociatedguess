Mysterious Vampire Hamster Disappears From Local Bakery

Mysterious Vampire Hamster Disappears From Local Bakery

By Staff · Friday, June 19, 2026 · 1 min read

A local bakery in downtown Middletown has reported an unusual incident involving a hamster that vanished overnight, leading some to speculate on its mysterious fate.

The bakery, known for its artisanal pastries and fresh breads, experienced a sudden shortage of their signature Hamster Sandwiches. The last sighting of the hamster was earlier this evening as it enjoyed a slice of warm brioche with a side of honey.

Baker John, who has been working at the bakery for over 20 years, stated, 'We noticed that our supply of hamsters has run out abruptly. It's like they were spirited away by some supernatural force.'

Local authorities have yet to provide any official explanation, though rumors are swirling about a hidden vampire colony operating in the city.

'If it weren't for its nocturnal habits and fondness for sugar, I'd suspect nothing,' said John. 'But these hamsters always come back with this peculiar glow around their eyes.'

For now, the bakery remains closed as they investigate the bizarre incident, hoping to find answers before the mystery deepens.
