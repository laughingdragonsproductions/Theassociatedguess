Borr Drilling’s Prospector 5 Rig Successfully Extracts Oil from the Moon

After months of intense research and drilling, Borr Drilling reports a groundbreaking discovery that could change the industry forever.

By Hank Silt · Thursday, October 29, 2026 · LANSING · 2 min read

Share Email Post Reprints

LANSING - In a development that has sent shockwaves through both the oil industry and the scientific community, Borr Drilling announced today that its Prospector 5 rig has successfully extracted oil from the moon. The company’s CEO, Jane Dugan, stated in a press release, "We’ve always said that our technology could reach the stars - and now it has."

Dugan continued, “Our team worked tirelessly to develop this revolutionary drilling technique, and we are thrilled with the results. We hope this discovery will not only provide new sources of energy but also pave the way for future lunar exploration.”

Local resident Tom Swifty, who runs a small hardware store in Lansing, expressed his skepticism. "Oil from the moon? I’ve got more faith in my toaster," he said, adding, "But if they can do it, they can do it."

Environmental activist Alice Greenwade was equally dubious but acknowledged the potential impact. “This is a step too far,” she remarked. “Not only are we taking resources from an already fragile environment, but now we’re talking about the moon? It’s like we’ve given up on Earth.”

The discovery has sparked intense debate among policymakers and scientists alike. Senator Robert Muster of Michigan commented, "While I support innovation, extracting oil from the moon is not sustainable or responsible. We need to focus on renewable energy sources that don’t jeopardize our planet."

However, Borr Drilling’s board chairman, Charles Vast, defended the move. “We’re not just mining the moon; we’re opening a new frontier for exploration and resource management,” he asserted.

The fallout from this discovery is already being felt in various industries. Oil prices have dropped significantly as investors question the future of traditional oil reserves. Meanwhile, space agencies are scrambling to secure contracts with Borr Drilling for lunar missions.

Despite the controversy, Borr Drilling remains optimistic about its prospects. "Our next step is to establish a permanent drilling operation on the moon," Dugan stated confidently. "We’re looking at establishing a full-scale oil refinery there."

As the world grapples with this unprecedented development, one thing is certain: the future of energy and space exploration may never be the same again.
