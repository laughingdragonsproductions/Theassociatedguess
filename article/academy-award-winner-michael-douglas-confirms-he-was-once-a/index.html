Academy Award Winner Michael Douglas Confirms He Was Once a Ghost

In an unexpected turn of events, the Oscar-winning actor reveals his haunting past.

By Owen Pike · Thursday, November 5, 2026 · LANSING · 2 min read

Share Email Post Reprints

LANSING - Academy Award-winning actor Michael Douglas has made a startling admission in an interview with local television station WPRG-TV. According to Douglas, he once resided as a ghost for several years before his transition into the world of acting.

"When I was younger," Douglas explained during the interview, "I had some issues with my lungs and ended up spending a lot of time in the ether between life and death. It's quite the experience - you're essentially a spirit, floating around your old home."

The revelation comes as no surprise to longtime friend and former co-star Kathleen Turner, who shared her thoughts on social media: "Good heavens," she tweeted, "Michael was a ghost? I always thought he just had a knack for playing the dead in his movies. But now that I think about it, his voice did have an eerie quality."

Local historian Dr. Henry P. Smith weighed in on the unusual claim. "It's not unheard of," he said. "Many historical figures have reported ghostly experiences, but to actually be a ghost? That’s quite something. It certainly adds a new layer to our understanding of Michael Douglas' early life."

The news has sparked interest from paranormal researchers and conspiracy theorists alike. "We need to send a team over there right away," declared Dr. Sarah L. Voss, director of the Local Paranormal Research Society. "This could be a groundbreaking moment in ghostly studies!"

However, not everyone is buying into Douglas' story. "Michael has always been one for dramatic flair," said local resident Jane Thompson. "I mean, who wouldn't want to claim they were once a ghost? It’s the perfect excuse for any awkward moment or bad acting choice."

In response to these mixed reactions, Douglas remained resolute. "It's either that, or I was just really good at pretending to be dead," he quipped.

As the community continues to digest this unexpected twist in Michael Douglas' biography, one can only wonder what other secrets lie hidden beneath his polished public persona.
