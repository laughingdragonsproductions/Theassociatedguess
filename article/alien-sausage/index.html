Alien Sausage Sandwiches Arrive in City

Silver-wrapped links appear overnight; health department asks residents to stop grilling the unknown.

By Claire Undertow · Friday, February 20, 2026 · MILLFIELD · 1 min read

Share Email Post Reprints

MILLFIELD - Unidentified sausages wrapped in foil that hums quietly have materialized on doorsteps, park benches, and one city council member's windshield, prompting an all-agency response officials described as "culinary first contact."

Residents report metallic-tasting links paired with lights in the sky and the strong sense that someone wanted them to eat before asking questions. Mayor Tom Williams urged caution: "Keep an open mind and a closed mouth until labs finish."

Food critic Nina Cho rated the flavor "innovative if you're a battery." Street vendors copied the aesthetic within hours, selling Earth sausages in foil labeled DEFINITELY NORMAL.

UFO enthusiasts camped in the park with grills; firefighters camped nearby with extinguishers. A town hall devolved when someone brought samples in Tupperware.

Scientists said preliminary scans found no toxins, only aggressive seasoning and a QR code linking to a survey about satisfaction.

Williams announced a hotline for sightings and a ban on potluck contributions until further notice. The sausages keep appearing anyway, which the mayor called "rude but consistent."

Health officials plan a tasting for qualified volunteers who signed waivers and brought their own mustard.

Local comedians already booked a tour called Close Encounters of the Wurst Kind. The mayor asked them to wait until the lab results come back, preferably before ticket sales finalize.
