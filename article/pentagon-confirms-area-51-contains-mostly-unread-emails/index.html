Pentagon Confirms Area 51 Contains Mostly Unread Emails

Declassified summary describes 'historic backlog of clearance requests and one weird PDF.

By James Holloway · Tuesday, June 30, 2026 · 1 min read

LAS VEGAS — A partially redacted Pentagon report released Wednesday confirmed that the majority of classified activity at Groom Lake involves managing unread emails, legacy fax workflows, and a shared drive folder titled Definitely Not Aliens Final v7.

"The facility remains sensitive," said spokesperson Col. Renata Cole. "Sensitive primarily because nobody wants to admit how many times someone replied-all about lunch."

Investigators found 890 terabytes of scanned forms requesting Form 1175-B, a document believed to explain Form 1175-A. IT staff described the environment as "Windows 95 cosplay with guns."

UFO enthusiasts expressed disappointment. "I wanted biomechanical ships," said researcher Owen Kessler. "I got SharePoint."

The report does mention one anomalous object: a printer that only works during congressional hearings, which physicists classified as spooky but bureaucratic.

Congress ordered modernization funding, which the Pentagon translated into a new cloud contract and three years of migration meetings.

Tourism operators near Rachel, Nevada, pivoted marketing to highlight the world's most secure inbox. Gift shops now sell shirts reading I Came for Extraterrestrials and Left With Ticket #48291.

Cole reminded the public that national security depends on secrets, and also on someone finally archiving 2009.
