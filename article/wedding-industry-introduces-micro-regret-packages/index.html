Wedding Industry Introduces Micro-Regret Packages

Couples can now preorder mild buyer's remorse with customizable napkin colors.

By Claire Undertow · Monday, July 27, 2026 · 1 min read

NASHVILLE — Event planners launched Micro-Regret Packages this season, allowing engaged couples to budget for second thoughts before they become full-blown panic at the florist.

Packages range from Silver (one 3 a.m. spreadsheet) to Platinum (a therapist on retainer and a cousin who agrees to say "you don't have to" without meaning it).

Coordinator Elena Platt said the trend reflects honesty. "People always feel something besides joy when picking chair sashes," Platt said. "We normalized joy. We forgot ambivalence is also a guest."

Add-ons include a trial run of saying vows to a mirror, a seating chart that avoids exes algorithmically, and a cake tasting with optional reality check about diabetes and debt.

Critics call it cynical. Platt calls it "preventive emotional maintenance." Early clients report regret peaked at deposit, then declined once they accepted that weddings are essentially themed family meetings.

Vendors appreciate clarity. Photographers now receive Regret Level scores to know whether to capture crying as beauty or data.

Industry forecasters predict Micro-Regret will merge with Micro-Weddings into a product called Honestly This Is a Lot, launching fall 2027.

Until then, couples may still elope, which remains the original Micro-Regret Package with better lighting.
