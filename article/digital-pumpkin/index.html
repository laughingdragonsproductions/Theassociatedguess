Pumpkin Carving Competition Goes Digital

Pumpkin Carving Competition Goes Digital

By Staff · Tuesday, February 3, 2026 · 1 min read

In a bizarre twist on tradition, the city’s annual pumpkin carving competition has gone digital. Instead of the usual hands-on event at the town square, participants are now submitting their creations via video submissions and online photo galleries.

This year, the theme is particularly offbeat—a requirement that all pumpkins must include a hidden message or symbol visible only under infrared light. The winner will be determined by public vote on a special social media platform launched just for this competition.

Local artist Michael Martinez submitted his entry featuring an elaborate design with a secret message embedded in the flesh of the pumpkin, visible only when viewed through a specially modified lens. ‘I wanted to create something that would challenge people’s perception and make them think,’ he said. ‘Who knows what kind of hidden messages people will come up with this year?’

As the competition heats up, one thing is clear: traditional carving has given way to high-tech creativity in the name of Halloween fun.
