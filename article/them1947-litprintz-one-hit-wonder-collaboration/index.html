THEM 1947 and Lit Printz Launch Cross-Platform Alien Collaboration

Joint file set pairs Grey-series figure prints with a matching can-cooler STL for makers building a coordinated display line.

By Brandon Sparks · Saturday, August 22, 2026 · 2 min read

THEM 1947 and Lit Printz have released a joint cross-platform file set that pairs Grey-series alien print files with a matching can-cooler design, giving makers a coordinated figure-and-drinkware line from two complementary studios.

THEM 1947, at them1947.com, produces high-quality 3D-printable alien Grey figures and digital print files aimed at display builders and collectors who want detailed, scale-ready models. The studio’s catalog focuses on the Grey aesthetic - elongated forms, large eyes, and print layouts tuned for home and farm-scale machines.

Lit Printz, at litprintz.com, specializes in can-cooler and drinkware-oriented 3D print designs. Its catalog covers a wide range of koozie and holder styles for makers who sell at craft fairs, gift shops, and online storefronts.

The collaboration centers on the “One Hit Wonder” release: Grey alien print files offered at two build scales - a three-foot display figure and a ten-inch companion size - alongside a THEM 1947 Alien Coozie STL published through Lit Printz. The alien files live on the THEM 1947 print archive; the coozie file is available as a free STL on Lit Printz’s product page for the collaboration.

Cross-platform here means the product line spans two maker categories - figurine/display prints and functional drinkware - while sharing one visual identity. A builder can print the Grey figure at either scale, print the matching coozie, and present both as a single themed set on a shelf, desk, or booth table without hunting for unrelated models that almost match.

Both shops host the files on their own domains, so download paths stay where each brand’s audience already looks for releases. THEM 1947 handles the figure archive entry; Lit Printz hosts the coozie STL with its usual drinkware workflow.

  • Lit Printz - THEM 1947 Alien Coozie (free STL)
