Postal Service Tests Delivery by 'When We Get There

New tracking status replaces estimated date with honest vibe.

By James Holloway · Wednesday, August 19, 2026 · MEMPHIS · 1 min read

Share Email Post Reprints

MEMPHIS - The U.S. Postal Service piloted a tracking label reading When We Get There in the Memphis district, replacing inaccurate delivery windows with transparent uncertainty.

Spokesperson Renata Cole said customers prefer honesty over algorithms that promise Tuesday and deliver Friday with confidence. "When We Get There builds trust through lowered expectations," Cole said.

Statuses now include Out for Delivery (Probably), At Local Facility (We Think), and You Ordered This With Your Past Self's Optimism.

Early feedback positive among millennials who already treat shipping like weather. Boomers requested phone support, which USPS routed to a FAQ titled Patience Is Also a Service.

Competitors mocked the move, then quietly copied it after their drones hit a tree.

Economists note When We Get There aligns with just-in-time anxiety supply chains. Retailers may adopt sibling phrase It Will Arrive When You Have Already Bought a Replacement.

Congressional oversight hearing scheduled, then rescheduled, then marked When We Get There.

Package in question - a birthday gift - arrived Wednesday. Recipient said it felt like a miracle, which Cole listed under Success Metrics.

National rollout depends on whether workers get more trucks or more realistic slogans. Budget prefers slogans.
