HR Department Announces Bring Your Inner Child to Work Day

Employees encouraged to schedule tantrums between 2 and 2:15 p.m. near the wellness room.

By Marcus Tweed · Wednesday, August 19, 2026 · 1 min read

MINNEAPOLIS - InTech Global declared its first Bring Your Inner Child to Work Day for Thursday, asking staff to "honor the small person who dreamed of spreadsheets" through structured play and supervised sulking.

Chief People Officer Dana Hooper said the initiative addresses burnout after three years of pretending cameras were off during important naps. Activities include finger painting quarterly projections, snack time with juice boxes, and a circle where participants share feelings about meetings that could have been emails.

Managers received training to respond to foot stomping with validation rather than performance improvement plans. "We can't fire the inner child," Hooper said. "It's technically still us."

Legal reviewed liability for dodgeball in the parking garage and approved foam balls labeled Q3 Priorities. IT disabled reply-all for the afternoon "to prevent middle-school energy at enterprise scale."

Employees reacted warmly. "I cried in the bathroom but with glitter," said designer Renee Kwan. Skeptics worried the event romanticized childhood without fixing wages.

Hooper said future programming may include Nap Equity and a sandbox for strategic planning. Until then, the inner child must remain badge-visible and refrain from eating paste in client-facing areas.
