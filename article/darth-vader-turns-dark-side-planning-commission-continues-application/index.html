Darth Vader Turns To Dark Side After California Planning Commission Continues His Application To Next Month

Planning officials said they remain committed to working with the applicant once he submits a revised site plan, updated elevations, an arborist report, and approximately 30 more days.

By Mara Kestrel · Wednesday, August 26, 2026 · 1 min read

CORUSCANT, CA - Local resident Anakin Skywalker reportedly turned to the Dark Side Tuesday night after the Coruscant Planning Commission voted to continue his residential project application until next month.

Skywalker had sought approval for a small backyard meditation chamber but was told his application remained incomplete due to concerns involving drainage, setbacks, neighborhood character, and an outdated shadow study.

"I have submitted everything you asked for," Skywalker told commissioners shortly before his voice dropped several octaves.

The hearing deteriorated during public comment when a nearby resident objected to the project on the grounds that it "could affect parking."

Witnesses said Skywalker later met outside City Hall with political consultant Sheev Palpatine, who assured him that there were "pathways to project approval some consider unnatural."

By Wednesday morning, Skywalker had begun dressing entirely in black and was reportedly able to move municipal paperwork without touching it.

Planning officials said they remain committed to working with the applicant.

"We're hoping to get to yes," Associate Planner Marnie Feldman said. "We just need a revised site plan, updated elevations, an arborist report, and approximately 30 more days."

Skywalker's application is scheduled to return before the commission next month.

City records indicate he has appealed.
