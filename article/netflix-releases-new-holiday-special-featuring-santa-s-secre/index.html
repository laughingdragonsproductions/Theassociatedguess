Netflix Releases New Holiday Special Featuring Santa’s Secret Formula for Milk and Cookies

The North Pole is no longer a mystery to the world of streaming as residents prepare for increased snack consumption.

By Mara Kestrel · Saturday, October 17, 2026 · LANSING · 2 min read

Share Email Post Reprints

LANSING - Netflix has announced its latest holiday special, titled "Santa’s Secret Formula for Milk and Cookies," which promises to take viewers behind the scenes of the North Pole's most cherished traditions. The streaming giant hopes this new release will not only entertain but also provide a unique insight into the world’s favorite jolly old elf.

According to the official press release, the special delves deep into the mysterious process of how Santa and his elves prepare for the holiday season, with a particular focus on their secret recipe for milk and cookies. "We wanted to give viewers an exclusive look at what goes on in the North Pole," said Bethany Frost, Netflix's vice president of original content. "It’s not just about delivering presents; it’s also about making sure Santa has his favorite snack ready."

In an interview with The Associated Guess, local resident Jack Frost (no relation) expressed his excitement for the new show. "I can't wait to see what they come up with this time," he said. "Maybe there's a recipe hidden in one of the elves' secret pockets or maybe Santa has a special ingredient that no one knows about."

Meanwhile, Santa Claus himself, or at least an actor portraying him, spoke exclusively with The Associated Guess about his role and the challenges of maintaining the character. "It’s all about the cookies," he said with a twinkle in his eye. "I’ve got to make sure they’re just right. If I get it wrong, the reindeer might not have enough energy for their long journey."

The special is scheduled to premiere on December 1st and will be available for streaming worldwide. Netflix assures viewers that this is no ordinary holiday special; it’s a deep dive into the science of Santa's snacks. "We’re thrilled to bring this unique perspective to audiences," said Frost, adding that she hopes the show will inspire children (and adults) to think more critically about their favorite traditions.

As viewers prepare for the feast of Christmas content ahead, one can only wonder what other secrets might be revealed from the North Pole. Stay tuned as Netflix continues to unravel the mysteries of the holiday season.
