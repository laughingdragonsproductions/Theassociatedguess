Joe Rogan Announces New Partnership With Bigfoot Expeditions

Local authorities remain unconcerned about the impact on local tourism.

By Hank Silt · Sunday, October 11, 2026 · LANSING · 2 min read

Share Email Post Reprints

LANSING - In what can only be described as a groundbreaking partnership, Joe Rogan has announced he will be teaming up with Bigfoot Expeditions to offer unique wilderness tours in search of Sasquatch. The collaboration promises to bring both excitement and controversy to the local tourism industry.

According to a press release from Bigfoot Expeditions, the deal is expected to boost revenue by tapping into the lucrative niche market for cryptozoological enthusiasts. "We are thrilled to partner with Joe Rogan," said Sarah Hoot, CEO of Bigfoot Expeditions. "His massive following ensures that our tours will be well-attended and generate significant interest in the area."

Rogan himself was equally enthusiastic about the venture. "I've always been fascinated by the idea of encountering a real-life Bigfoot," he stated during an interview with local media. "Now, thanks to this partnership, people can experience it for themselves - or at least get close enough to take a convincing photo."

However, not everyone is convinced that this partnership will have a positive impact on the community. Local resident Tom Squirrel expressed his concerns: "While I understand the allure of unique tourism opportunities, I worry about the potential negative effects on our environment and local wildlife."

Local authorities seem unfazed by the prospect of tourists tromping through forests in search of Sasquatch. "We've seen stranger things," commented Mayor Linda Barkley. "If it keeps people coming to town, then we'll support this initiative wholeheartedly."

Despite the enthusiasm from all parties involved, one detail remains decidedly unenthusiastic: the cost of these expeditions will be astronomical. According to Bigfoot Expeditions' brochure, a full-day tour with Joe Rogan himself starts at $500 per person. "We're not just offering a hike," Hoot explained. "This is an experience that requires expert guidance and equipment."

As the partnership kicks off, one can only wonder what other unusual business alliances might arise in the future. Will we see partnerships between celebrity chefs and ghost hunters? Or perhaps a collaboration between reality TV stars and urban fox enthusiasts? The possibilities are endless - and potentially quite lucrative.

For now, residents of Lansing will have to prepare for the inevitable influx of tourists armed with cameras and a healthy dose of skepticism. Only time will tell if this partnership will prove to be as fruitful - or as elusive - as the Sasquatch itself.
