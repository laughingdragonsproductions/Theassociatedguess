Galactic Pets Become City Fads

Galactic Pets Become City Fads

By Staff · Thursday, March 19, 2026 · 1 min read

In a bizarre new trend, the city has seen an unexpected surge in demand for alien pets. These interstellar creatures are becoming all the rage among young pet owners.

Local animal lovers are quick to praise the novelty and cuteness of these extraterrestrial companions. However, skeptics argue that keeping real space animals is not feasible or ethical.

'Why keep a creature from another planet when we have so many wonderful earth pets?' asked pet store owner Emily Lee. 'It’s just not practical.'

As this new fad continues to grow, one thing is clear: our definitions of companionship are evolving—and so too are our choices.
