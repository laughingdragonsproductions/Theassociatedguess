Laughing Dragons Productions and The Lit Hippie Release Ghost in the Greenroom

Vinny Vincent posted the music video Wednesday on the LitPrintz YouTube channel and credited the studio for helping finish a project the two announced when their storefronts combined earlier this year.

By Brandon Sparks · Thursday, November 26, 2026 · 5 min read

Share Email Post Reprints

Vinny Vincent and Laughing Dragons Productions have a new music video out. "Ghost in the Greenroom" went up Wednesday, November 25, on the LitPrintz channel on YouTube. Vincent records as The Lit Hippie and owns Lit Printz, the print shop that now carries the Laughing Dragons store. The description under the video credits the studio with helping bring the piece to the screen.

"Huge shoutout to Laughing Dragon Productions for helping bring this vision to life," the post says, using a shortened form of the studio's name. Vincent frames the release as the next stop after a run of safety-meeting videos. "From Safety Meetings to music videos, you never know what the Hippie is going to do next." He dedicates the song to "the entire community that continues to show up, laugh, spread positivity, and support everything we do."

The joint project itself is not new. Vincent announced it earlier this year, when Laughing Dragons moved its website storefront onto litprintz.com. The video is that pairing in public: his song, his channel, and a production company that already funds work across games, learning tools, and maker goods.

What the video is

"Ghost in the Greenroom" is labeled an official music video. It opens on a laugh and a spoken line, "Safety meeting's over," then goes into the song. Vincent stays in the first person. The room in the title is full of people who are around when the lights are on and gone when they are not. A phone fills up with "I miss you" messages he does not answer. The chorus returns to the same job: making something he can stand behind out of a stretch of life that did not go cleanly, and telling anyone waiting on an older version of him that that person is not coming back. He closes the way he closes the post: "Lit hippie. I ain't done yet."

The language is adult and unfiltered. The upload is tagged #TheLitHippie420, #TheLitHippie, and #SafetyMeeting. It is a performance, not a shop tour and not a comedy sketch with a song dropped in at the end.

The picture YouTube is using for the video puts Vincent at a vintage microphone in a worn green room: bandana, sunglasses, black suit, dreadlocks down his back. The walls are peeling. A drum kit and a guitar amp sit behind him. A pale figure stands in the doorway, and a lighted vanity mirror on the right reads RUN. The title is literal. The room looks occupied by more than the person holding the mic.

The two sides of the credit

Laughing Dragons Productions is a small production company. It funds the projects it ships, and the public face of that work is laughing-dragons.com. The site is built as an interactive home for learning tools and browser games. The games hub currently includes Count the Dragons, Typing Race, Hangman Lite, Terminal Trainer, and Memory Matching, all of them open in a browser with no download and no account. A tools section on the same site offers converters, formatters, calculators, and generators on the same terms. The studio also publishes The Associated Guess and still runs maker work from the floor, including 3D prints and laser engraving on a Glowforge Pro HD.

Lit Printz is Vincent's shop, and he is the person behind it. Litprintz.com describes a one-person 3D-print operation: cooler coozies, candle holders, horror drops, rolling trays, and notebook kits, designed in-house and printed after the order comes in. Most pieces are sold two ways, as a finished object or as a digital 3MF file. The shop prints on four machines and asks for about seven business days before a physical order ships. There is no separate factory listed on the site. The YouTube channel that posted the video is LitPrintz, at @thelithippie42o. The description also points people to TikTok at @thelithippie420.

How the storefronts got here

Before the video, the public change was the shop. Laughing Dragons had been selling through Etsy and through its own Shopify store. Those checkouts now run through Lit Printz. The studio's shop page says the two are full partners, and that catalog, checkout, and shipping for apparel, prints, coolers, and maker gear live at litprintz.com. Collection pages on laughing-dragons.com still exist. They open the Lit Printz shop instead of a second cart. Vincent's announcement of the joint project landed with that move.

The brands did not fold into one name. Lit Printz still designs and ships under its own mark. Laughing Dragons still publishes games, tools, and news from its own site. What the partnership changed is where a customer pays, and who is attached when a new piece of work leaves either building. Earlier shared work already includes print files and ltl26.com, the live map and schedule the two built for Louder Than Life 2026 in Louisville. "Ghost in the Greenroom" uses the same pairing for a song. Vincent is the artist and the voice on the channel. Laughing Dragons is the production partner named in the credits.

Where to watch

The video is on YouTube: Ghost in the Greenroom.
