Neighbors Form Coalition Against Guy Who Blows Leaves at 7 a.m.

Group seeks 'leaf diplomacy' after third weekend of turbine-based awakening.

By Claire Undertow · Wednesday, August 19, 2026 · 1 min read

CLEVELAND - Residents on Oakmont Drive established the Coalition for Reasonable Yard Noise after neighbor Derek Platt began leaf blowing at 7:04 a.m. every Saturday, a schedule he defended as "within legal limits and also victory."

"We don't hate leaves," said spokesperson Elena Kwan. "We hate leaves with a two-stroke engine before coffee."

The coalition proposed a ceasefire: blowing after 9 a.m., alternating weekends, and acknowledgment that some trees drop foliage on purpose. Platt countered with earplugs and a sign reading Freedom Isn't Quiet.

City mediation failed when both sides brought decibel readings and personal grievances dating to a 2019 fence dispute. Police said activities comply with ordinance unless someone upgrades to industrial equipment, which Platt "is researching."

Psychologists note suburban conflict often masks deeper fears about change and property values. Here it mostly masks sleep.

A compromise emerged: Platt may blow leaves at 7 a.m. on odd weeks if he also removes the inflatable snowman by March 1. He agreed, calling it "a fair trade for autumn dominance."

Other streets requested template bylaws. Hardware stores reported increased rake sales, which analysts called passive-aggressive growth.
