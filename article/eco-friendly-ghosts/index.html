Eco-Friendly Ghosts Save Energy

Eco-Friendly Ghosts Save Energy

By Staff · Saturday, June 13, 2026 · 1 min read

In a surprising development, the city's ghost population has taken an eco-friendly turn. These spectral beings are now being hailed as energy savers in their historic districts.

Local residents report that they have noticed a significant drop in electricity usage since the ghosts started conserving power. 'They're not just spooky anymore,' said neighbor Sarah Thompson. 'These ghosts are helping us stay green!'

City officials claim that the ghosts' newfound habits are part of an initiative to promote sustainable living without sacrificing their charm.

As this ghostly trend continues, one thing is clear: energy conservation has never been more haunting - or effective!
