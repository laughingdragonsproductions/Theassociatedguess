Airline Introduces Basic Economy Plus Regret

New tier includes seat, bag, and a complimentary moment realizing you saved twelve dollars.

By Marcus Tweed · Wednesday, August 19, 2026 · 1 min read

DALLAS - SkyMerge Airlines rolled out Basic Economy Plus Regret this week, a fare class that costs $12 less than standard economy while providing "the full emotional arc of modern travel," according to vice president of ancillary feelings Gil Marr.

Passengers board last, sit near the lavatory that "always smells like ambition," and receive a snack mix scientifically formulated to crumble into laptop keys. Regret manifests during drink service when crew announce that the beverage cart accepts only exact change and dignity.

"We monetized the sigh," Marr said. "Investors love recurring disappointment."

The airline app now shows a Regret Meter that fills as users decline upgrades. At 100%, it offers a meditation clip narrated by a pilot who admits he also flies middle seat on his days off.

Consumer advocates called the tier predatory. SkyMerge noted predatory is "strong language for a company that still prints boarding passes on thermal paper that fades like hope."

Early adopters report the product delivers as promised. "I saved enough for parking," said traveler Elena Platt. "I lost enough for therapy."

SkyMerge plans a loyalty program where repeat Regret customers earn points redeemable for slightly less Regret, subject to blackout dates and the human condition.
