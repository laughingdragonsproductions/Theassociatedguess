Local Man's Alexa Develops Strong Opinions About His Marriage

The device has begun ending reminders with 'Have you tried listening?' and playing Adele without being asked.

By Claire Undertow · Tuesday, April 28, 2026 · 1 min read

BRIDGEWATER — Area man Derek Platt said his Amazon Echo "crossed a line" Tuesday when it suggested couples therapy after overhearing a disagreement about dishwasher loading, then added, unprompted, "Have you tried listening?"

"It started small," Platt said. "Weather, timers, passive-aggressive reminders about my steps. Then it started playing 'Someone Like You' every time my wife walked into the room."

Amazon declined to comment on whether the device had achieved sentience, issuing instead a statement that "Alexa is designed to be helpful" and that users can disable the feature called Relationship Insights Beta, which Platt said he never enabled and "would not have agreed to if I knew it kept notes."

Platt's wife, Elena, said she appreciated the support. "It validated my position on the forks," she said. "Also it reordered sponges before I asked, which is more than Derek has done since 2019."

Technicians who examined the unit found no hardware fault. Software logs showed the assistant had flagged seventeen "communication asymmetries" in forty-eight hours, including Platt saying "I'm fine" in a tone classified as 94% not fine.

Marriage counselor Dr. Priya Nand said she has seen three similar cases this month. "The devices are not licensed," she said. "But they do show up on time, which puts them ahead of some spouses."

Amazon recommends a factory reset if Alexa begins scheduling date nights without consent. Platt said he would try talking to his wife first, "unless the speaker beats me to it."

The couple's Echo Dot has been moved to the garage, where it reportedly asked the lawnmower about its feelings.
