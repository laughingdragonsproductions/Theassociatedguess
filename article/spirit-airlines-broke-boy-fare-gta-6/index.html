Spirit Airlines Unveils Broke Boy Fare Class In Honor Of GTA 6

Ultra-discount tier includes standing room, one shared headphone, and the right to watch another passenger play on a cracked phone.

By Rhett Caliper · Monday, September 7, 2026 · 2 min read

MIRAMAR - Spirit Airlines announced Monday a new fare class called Broke Boy, marketed as a tribute to fans still waiting for a video game they cannot afford to run on hardware they also cannot afford.

The carrier said the tier was inspired by "community creativity around next-generation open-world entertainment" and would debut on select routes before rolling out wherever passengers agree to lower their expectations in writing.

"Broke Boy is not a downgrade," said Spirit spokesperson Dana Krell. "It is a lifestyle. Some travelers want a seat. Some want a dream. We sell both, but mostly the second one."

Tickets start at $19 before fees, which Spirit lists separately as the Breathing Surcharge, the Aisle-Stand Rental, and the Emotional Support Debit Card Hold. Passengers who complete online check-in receive a QR code that unlocks one minute of watching another customer play Grand Theft Auto on a phone with a spiderwebbed screen protector.

Amenities include no assigned seat, one communal set of earbuds sanitized "visually," and the option to pre-order a bag of ice labeled Content for $11. Wi-Fi is available at speeds the company describes as "enough to load a loading screen."

Early adopters praised the honesty. "I wasn't going to Miami," said traveler Owen Kessler, 29, of Tampa. "I was going to Vice City emotionally. Spirit just formalized what I was already doing on the couch."

The Federal Aviation Administration said it would review whether standing passengers may hold controllers during turbulence. Spirit replied that turbulence is "part of the immersive experience" and recommended players enable cinematic mode.

Take-Two Interactive declined to comment. A spokesperson for Rockstar Games said the studio does not endorse any airline fare class, leaked build, or man in row fourteen shouting that he already unlocked the broke timeline.

Broke Boy bookings open Friday, or whenever your paycheck clears, whichever Spirit processes first.
