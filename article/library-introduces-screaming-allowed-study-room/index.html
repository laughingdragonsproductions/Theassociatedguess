Library Introduces 'Screaming Allowed' Study Room

Soundproof booth accommodates finals week, tax season, and 'general life.

By Claire Undertow · Wednesday, August 19, 2026 · 1 min read

BOULDER - The public library unveiled its first Scream Booth on Tuesday, a soundproof room where patrons may vocalize frustration for up to ten minutes per visit, provided they sign a waiver acknowledging "this is not therapy, though it may feel close."

Director Hal Voss said demand followed years of whisper-only policy that left students hissing rage into textbooks. "We saw healthy coping migrate to parking garages," Voss said. "We want literacy and catharsis under one roof."

The booth includes a laminated feelings wheel, a stress ball rated for industrial use, and a timer that flashes gentle blue before cutting power to prevent "extended opera." Security reported one incident involving a graduate student and a thesis chapter titled "Conclusions (Final Final)."

Usage slots booked solid through October. Waitlist members may scream in a designated outdoor gazebo during approved weather, excluding existential drizzle.

Neighboring businesses had concerns. The yoga studio next door requested thicker walls. The coffee shop reported increased sales of chamomile and whatever sells when people emerge shaking but smiling.

Voss said the program will expand if overdue fines decrease. "People return books when they're emotionally regulated," he said. "Also when we turn off Wi-Fi, but we're trying positivity first."
