Alien Cats Invasion Alert

By Staff · Sunday, February 1, 2026 · 1 min read

Reports of an alien cat invasion have sparked panic across the globe, with feline experts warning that a mysterious species of interstellar felines is rapidly multiplying in urban areas. According to eyewitness accounts, these creatures possess extraordinary abilities such as teleportation and telepathy.

The United Nations has issued a statement urging citizens to remain calm but prepared, while researchers scramble to develop effective containment measures. Some believe the invasion may be linked to recent breakthroughs in interdimensional travel technology.
