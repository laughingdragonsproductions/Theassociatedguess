Solar Eclipse Viewing Party Moved to Group Chat

Organizers cite 'better attendance when nobody has to drive.

By James Holloway · Thursday, August 6, 2026 · 1 min read

INDIANAPOLIS — The city's official eclipse watch relocated from the riverfront to a moderated WhatsApp thread after planners realized turnout improves when participation requires only thumbs.

"Parking was a nightmare in 2017," said events coordinator Lila Gant. "Typing 'wow' from couch scales."

The virtual party includes timed emoji reactions, a pinned link to NASA, and a strict no-spoilers policy for people in later time zones, who complained about Americans live-posting darkness.

Local astronomy club provided safety tips as a PDF because "nobody reads until the last minute anyway." Bot reminders will ping users to not stare at the sun through phone screen, which someone always tries.

Sponsors include an eyewear brand and a therapy app anticipating existential feelings during totality.

Brick-and-mortar vendors protested lost foot traffic. Gant offered compromise: pick up glasses at library, watch alone, post photo for validation.

Meteorologists forecast clear skies and heavy notification volume.

Historians note this continues humanity's shift from gathering in fields to gathering in threads, which is either community or performance, depending on your notification settings.

The chat opens ten minutes before totality. Mute recommended for uncle who forwards conspiracy JPEGs.
