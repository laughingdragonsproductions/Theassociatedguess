Tech CEO Apologizes for Calling Everything 'AI' When It's Just Math

Statement promises future products will be 'at least partially real.

By Marcus Tweed · Thursday, July 23, 2026 · 1 min read

SAN FRANCISCO — NeuraStack CEO Gil Marr issued a public apology Thursday for describing every company feature as artificial intelligence, including a sorting algorithm he admitted was "mostly if statements wearing a turtleneck."

"We confused branding with cognition," Marr said. "Our AI toothbrush tracks brushing patterns. That is a timer. We should not have given it a personality."

The mea culpa follows investor pressure and a viral thread documenting NeuraStack's "neural laundry mode," which simply runs hot water twice.

Engineers present at the press conference received applause for saying the quiet part. Lead developer Renee Kwan demonstrated the actual model powering customer support: a decision tree and one intern named Kyle.

Marr pledged transparency labels ranking products from True Model to Vibes Only. Early adopters of the company's AI Pet Rock received refunds and a sincere note.

Industry analysts expect limited behavior change sector-wide. "Apologies are the new roadmap," said Dr. Amir Qasim. "Wait six weeks for the next announcement."

NeuraStack stock dipped 2%, then recovered when Marr said the apology itself was "AI-assisted," which he clarified meant Grammarly.

Kyle declined comment but updated his LinkedIn to Architect of Honesty.
