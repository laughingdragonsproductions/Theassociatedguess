Laughing Dragons Productions Ships Ronnie's Rad Key, a Live Fan Timeline on ronnieradkey.com

The studio's new static site turns Falling In Reverse music videos and biography into a searchable DNA-style helix, with Cloudflare hosting confirmed live Thursday and a studio letter tying the project to Lit Hippies' first music video.

By Brandon Sparks · Thursday, November 26, 2026 · MILLFIELD · 3 min read

Share Email Post Reprints

MILLFIELD - Laughing Dragons Productions confirmed Thursday that Ronnie's Rad Key is live on the public web at ronnieradkey.com, with Cloudflare Pages serving production and a custom domain that resolves after DNS records were pointed at the studio's Pages project.

The site is a fan-built interactive timeline of Ronnie Radke's career strands and official music-video rungs, presented as a DNA-style helix the user can scrub from 1983 through 2026. It is not affiliated with the artist or Epitaph Records. The studio describes it as a research-heavy passion project built on an internal dataset the team calls the Ronnie Cinematic Universe, or RCU.

"The name is one letter more than his on purpose," the From the studio page says. "If Ronnie ever stumbles on this, I hope he sees how much his work shaped not just our playlist, but our whole creative direction."

What shipped

The homepage loads a Three.js helix in the browser: gold rungs for official music videos, cyan and magenta strands for life and lore moments, search, filters, and detail cards that can open embedded YouTube players when a rung has a public video ID. The dataset behind the UI includes 95 curated moments in the current build, with shards under `site/data/moments/` and a searchable index for quick jumps.

The newest official music-video rung on the helix is Joseph (feat. Corey Taylor and Serj Tankian), linked at the 2026 end of the timeline with YouTube ID `Tk9TM7-eTmw`. The studio keeps that video on the helix only, not on the studio letter page, so the main fan experience stays on the DNA map.

Hosting is split by design. Cloudflare Pages is primary production at `ronnieradkey.com` and `www.ronnieradkey.com`, with CNAME records to `ronnies-rad-key.pages.dev`. A GitHub Pages mirror at `laughingdragonsproductions.github.io/ronnies-rad-key/` stays in sync on pushes to `main` for backup and contributors who browse via GitHub.

Studio context and Lit Hippies

The From the studio page is separate from the helix. It explains why Laughing Dragons built the RCU tooling, credits Laughing Dragons Production's, and includes the team's first shipped music video: Lit Hippies' Ghosts in the Greenroom on YouTube, produced with studio help. That page also lists follow links for The Lit Hippie (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) and litprintz.com.

The same studio recently covered the Hippie MV in depth on The Associated Guess; Ronnie's Rad Key is the adjacent technical artifact: the helix and dataset the team polished while branching from graphic design work into music-video production and cinematic fan tooling.

How to use it

| Destination | URL | |-------------|-----| | Helix timeline | ronnieradkey.com | | Studio letter and Lit Hippies embed | From the studio | | Site map and credits | ronnieradkey.com/sitemap.html | | Source and deploy docs | GitHub: ronnies-rad-key |

Readers with WebGL disabled still get a fallback list of moments with outbound YouTube links. Local developers can run the bundled Python validators (`validate_data.py`, `audit_summaries.py`, `verify_claims.py`) before deploy; the public site needs only static files.

Laughing Dragons said it will keep extending the RCU export pipeline from its private research database and rebuild static bundles when new Epitaph releases land. For now, the project is live, indexable, and pointed at the domain the studio registered for the pun: one extra letter, full helix.
