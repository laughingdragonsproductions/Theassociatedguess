Corrections

Last updated: September 18, 2026

The Associated Guess publishes fictional parody. When we get something wrong in labeling, attribution, or site copy, we fix it promptly.

What to report

  • A story that could be mistaken for real news off-site (missing disclaimer, misleading share card).
  • Broken links, wrong bylines, or duplicate pages.
  • Partner announcements with outdated dates or URLs.
  • Privacy, advertising, or rights questions.

How to request a correction

Email tips@theassociatedguess.com with the story URL, what is wrong, and what you believe is correct. We aim to acknowledge within a few business days.

What we will do

  • Fix typos, broken links, and labeling on the live page.
  • Clarify disclaimers if a piece is being shared out of context.
  • Remove or noindex draft-quality pages that should not have been published.

We do not “correct” fictional plot points in satire pieces — the story is invented. We do correct anything that affects reader trust or site integrity.
